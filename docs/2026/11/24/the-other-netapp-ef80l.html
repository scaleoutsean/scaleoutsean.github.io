    The other NetApp EF80L

    2026-11-24 00:00 less than 1 minute read

    On this page

    Here's the NetApp EF80L, in which "L" means Lustre.

    How about this?

    • An EF80L where L stands for Linux with the optional small Kubernetes distribution such as k0rdent k0?
    • TopoLVM or my SANtricity CSI
    • Rook (Ceph on Kubernetes) with EF Series or just Ceph on bare metal, if you don't want Ceph on Kubernetes
    • Rook Storage Classes with RAID 0 or RAID 6 for data that works okay with RF3 and Erasure Coding. SCs on RAID10 for natively-replicated databases (CNPG, etc.)
    • Lightweight compression in Ceph and other filesystems to avoid waste created by inefficient data
    • Containers, VMs, light databases, S3, and more
    • Velero for backup/restore, including built-in Changed Block Tracking once they figure it out, as it's not even clear after v1.19 which CSI is supported (if any)

    It wouldn't be the same, but could it be good for general workloads? I think so.
